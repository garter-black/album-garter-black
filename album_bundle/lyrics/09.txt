[Intro - spoken, half-whisper over sparse piano]
Nah, for real — sit down a second. Let me tell you about my boss.
Everybody out here callin' him self-made, like he pulled it off solo.
I'm in the next room. I see the receipts. That ain't the story.
The story's got a hundred names in it — and not one of 'em is only his.
So check it.

[Verse 1]
He came in swingin' at the odds like he could box 'em on his own,
"nobody's comin', do it solo" — that's the script he carved in stone.
But every single time the floor dropped out — and trust, it dropped a lot —
somebody he'd half-forgotten showed and caught him on the spot.
He went chasin' "self-made," tryna prove he'd never need a hand,
and woke up a charity case — and that's the part he understands.
He'll tell you flat: there's no such man, that self-made thing's a myth;
the almost-died-then-someone-saved-him bit? Too constant. It's a gift.

[Hook]
They put an X on him — the kind that's supposed to mean you're through,
the kind they stamp on the throwaways and swear there's nothin' you can do.
But every X that should've sunk him flipped into a check,
a little yes, a little "go on," a debt he won't forget.
So it's thank you, thank you, to the ones they wrote off as a wreck —
the X that should've been a no became the best yes he ever kept.

[Verse 2]
And peep who had his back the most — the exact ones you're told to avoid:
the neurodivergent, the "diagnosed," the wiring they call paranoid.
A narcissist that's up at dawn just textin' him "you good? you eat?",
struts like he owns the mirror — but the mirror's checkin' on his teammate.
A psychopath broke down in tears, said "I am not watchin' you fall,"
took that flat, switched-off, ice-cold heart and spent it keepin' my boss tall.
A borderline went nuclear — the whole floor felt the blast —
and still, mid-detonation, threw her body 'tween him and the glass.

[Verse 3]
You got autistic minds and allistic minds both crackin' the other's code,
dead-set on a mission just to meet each other halfway down the road,
two operating systems, no translator, no manual, no map,
runnin' on raw stubborn patience and a love that won't hang up at the gap.
A "lazy" guy — his word, not mine — is grindin' through a whole PhD
to drag down sixty-seven thousand deaths a year and set somebody free.
A so-called sociopath out here dealin' the broke a better deck:
fifty-five ways to stack a dollar, lowest risk-to-check.

[Bridge]
There's a project every expert swore would never see a cent,
"no version of this profits" — every spreadsheet, every consultant.
A hundred days he wrestled it, no sleep, no floor, no bed,
and the thing that couldn't make a dime turned green instead.
Four K, forty, four hundred, then a milli in the black —
and he points around the room like "none of this was me — look back."
Thankful — that's the word he keeps tattooed across his chest:
the man who should've sunk alone got carried by the rest.

[Verse 4 / Outro - strip it down, just piano and her]
But here's the part that wrecks me, where the whole thing finally lands:
every miracle I just ran off got set in motion by a kid with no hands.
A little soul they called X — never breathed, never grew, never came,
gone at four months in the dark... and look — look what he became.
He wasn't even born, okay? Man, he wasn't even here.
And he moved a hundred broken people to go wipe each other's tears.
So keep your self-made fairy tale — my boss'll take the wreck,
the loss that loved him back to life, the X that turned into a check.
He'll spend the rest of his days bein' the echo of a kid who never spoke —
the X that should've been a no... the X that should be a check.
