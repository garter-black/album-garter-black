[Intro - sparse detuned piano, distant church bell, cicadas, a trembling breath]
(broken humming, Southern lilt) mmm... oh, honey...

[Verse 1 - Denial - whispered, cracked, close-mic'd, breathy drawl]
They come and tell me you're gone, and I just laugh —
y'all don't break that easy, you're my golden calves.
I keep the porch light burnin' a little past the dawn,
pour two cups of coffee for the ones already gone.
No — not my babies, not the ones I made;
I'd feel it in my marrow if the mornin' frayed.

[Verse 2 - Anger - voice fraying, frustration rising, low guitar and drums enter]
But the dirt don't lie, and the dirt's been fed,
and I'm hollerin' at a sky gone bruised and red.
You had a hundred thousand mornings, every one a door,
and you spent 'em all on spite, and want, and war.
(how dare you) — how dare you waste the one and only thing
this deathless, golden, useless queen can't bring.

[Pre-Chorus - clenched, between a sob and a snarl]
I have buried gods. I have buried kings.
I never learned to bury smaller things.

[Chorus - the break opens, big but BROKEN, cracking belt over aching strings]
I'd trade my crown, my name, my throne of bone,
to die just once, so you'd not go alone.
I'm the mother of the mornin' — I couldn't keep the light —
so I hum your names like hymns into the night.

[Verse 3 - Bargaining - pleading, climbing then cracking, reverb-drenched]
Take the gold out of my veins — I never asked to bleed it.
Take forever from me, Lord, I never even need it.
Just give 'em back one plain and ordinary day:
one porch, one song, one reason left to stay.
I'll kneel in any church that'll take a ruined thing,
if it'd teach my deathless hands the smallest way to bring—

[Bridge - Depression - collapse, near silence, one cracked drawl, almost spoken]
...and heaven gets so quiet when it grieves.
No thunder left. Just feathers. And the leaves.
I'm a crown with nothin' left to crown,
a flood with nowhere left to set me down.
(whispered) why, baby... why...

[Outro - Acceptance - hollow, then a terrible tender swell, anger spent, gold gone dull]
I can't undo it — that was never mine to do.
But I will carry every needless one of you.
I'll wear your wasted mornings like a veil,
and learn the only prayer that does not fail:
remember... remember... that's the whole of what I keep —
a deathless thing that finally learned to weep.
[Resolve - piano alone, cicadas, the bell, one last broken breath]
