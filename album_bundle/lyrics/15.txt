[Intro - close-mic detuned piano, vinyl crackle, faint room tone, a long broken breath]
(soft, trembling hum)

[Verse 1 - hushed, numb, surrendering, close-mic'd]
The lights got cut on Tuesday — I didn't even flinch,
counted out my last in nickels, came up short an inch.
A funeral, a funeral, then one more borrowed dress;
I stopped foldin' my own laundry, stopped cleanin' up the mess.

[Verse 2 - the weight, voice fraying]
The doctor's got a word for me, the pharmacy's got three,
and not a one of 'em can spell how tired I came to be.
I buried everybody — then I went and lost my name,
and the quiet got so heavy that the silence felt the same.

[Pre-Chorus - the surrender; sounds like a goodbye]
So I left the back door open, let the cold come walkin' through.
Pull up a chair, old friend — I know it's finally you.
I'm not gonna run this time. I'm not gonna make a sound.
I've been ready a long while now...

[Chorus - the TURN: don't announce it; the trembling just stops; soft heartbeat enters]
...funny — my hands just went still.
First time in years they ain't shakin'. Guess they never will.
You came here for a victim, sweetheart. Sit down. Stay a while.
See, I have lost so much, so often...
I forgot how to lose.
Not tonight. Not like this. Not you.

[Verse 3 - predatory calm; never name it; the listener senses she's holding something]
You walked me through each funeral. You showed me every face.
You never once considered I was learnin' how you move.
They took the house, the meds, the name, the light —
but there's one small thing they never found... and I saved it for tonight.
(whispered) I never told a soul. I never made a sound.

[Bridge - intimate, certain]
I'm still afraid. I'm still undone.
But "afraid" and "finished" were never the same one.
I don't need to win, sweetheart — I just need a sliver. A shot.
And a sliver of a shot is so much more than the world swore I'd got.

[Outro - hush, the smallest hope, steady]
So the light stays off. The dress stays black.
But I'm sittin' up now. And I'm sittin' back.
One small thing. One small "maybe."
One more mornin'... and that's the thread that holds me.
(soft, steady) Not today. ...Not today.
