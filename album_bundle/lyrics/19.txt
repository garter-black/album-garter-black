[Intro - cold wind, distant bell, lone acoustic guitar, a single breath]
[Soprano vocalise, far away, pure head voice: aaah...]

[Verse - Denial: hushed, floating, breathy soprano, pp]
No — not the children of my golden sky,
they would not break what even gods can't buy.
I only dreamed the ash, the emptied throne,
the marble cities calling, "come back home."
(tell me I am sleeping)
tell me none of it is true,
tell me morning keeps the promises I drew.

[Pre-Chorus - Anger rising: drums enter, strings tighten, voice darkens]
But the smoke does not lie,
and the dead do not rise,
and I taste it now — the speed of pain behind my eyes.

[Chorus - Anger: huge, wrathful, full chest, choir stabs, distorted guitar]
How dare you spend your one and only breath
on spite, on greed, on manufactured death.
A universe of mornings — and you chose
the knife, the crown, the cruelty no one chose.
(how dare you... how dare you...)

[Instrumental break - strings and organ, storm receding]

[Verse - Bargaining: church organ, pleading, vibrato blooms]
Take my crown. Take my deathless, hollow years.
Give them back one sunrise. Dry the children's tears.
I will trade Olympus, every golden door,
for a single hour no one suffers anymore.
[Build]
Please — I am a queen with nothing left to reign,
just let me buy them back from needless pain.

[Bridge - Depression: collapse, near silence, sparse piano, cracked grief]
...and heaven is so quiet when it grieves.
No thunder left. Just feathers. And the leaves.
(whispered) I have no throne, no sky, no name —
only the weight of watching, and the shame.

[a cappella - fragile soprano, single voice, almost spoken]
why... why... why...

[Outro - Acceptance: tempo lifts, towering operatic climax, full orchestra + choir]
I cannot save you — that was never mine to keep,
but I will hold the flame for every soul asleep.
I will sing your needless sorrow into name,
so no one born hereafter dies the same.
[Climax - soprano soars, sustained high note, choir swells]
[Latin choir, soft] dona eis requiem
rest now... rest now... the queen of heaven weeps,
and turns your speed of pain to something that she keeps.
[Resolve - strings fade, lone bell, last pure soprano note, a breath]
