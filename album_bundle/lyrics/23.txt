[Intro: solo piano, gentle and aching, intimate female voice]
There's an empty chair where my brother used to laugh
A coffee going cold and an old coat on the door
He felt the whole world louder than the rest of us could bear
And nobody had taught us what the quiet was for

[Verse 1]
He saw the colours brighter, felt the edges sharper too
Born with a heart the size of weather in a world that never knew
We said "calm down," we said "stop making it so hard"
Like you'd tell a man with no legs to run
He was never broken, he was wired a little different
And different was never something to outrun

[Pre-Chorus]
And oh, where, oh where did the warning signs all go?
We were looking right at him, God, we should have known

[Chorus: building, anthemic, aching]
He was somebody's brother, somebody's whole entire world
Not a headline, not a number, not a "should have been more careful" word
If anybody, anybody had only understood
He'd be here, and that's the part that breaks me good
He was somebody's brother
And it did not have to end

[Verse 2]
Three thousand empty chairs in this country every year
Near seven hundred thousand candles that the whole wide world should hear
And the ones who looked away, who said "it's not my place"
the silence is a hand that lets them go
You don't have to be a doctor, you just have to be a witness
You just have to stop and answer, let them know

[Bridge: the thesis, swelling]
It's not their job to be easy for the rest of us to read
It's ours to learn their language, ours to meet a different need
Understanding runs both ways, it's the bridge we never built
And shame on all the quiet, 'cause the silence shares the guilt

[Final Chorus: hopeful turn, full band and choir, a call to act]
So if you're the one in the dark tonight, still here, still holding on,
stay, 'cause the world gets so much colder the second that you're gone
One three, one one, one four, there's a voice on the other end
You are somebody's brother, you are somebody's friend
We could save them by the thousands if we only learned to listen
Say their name, learn their language, break the silence
Stay, stay, stay

[Outro: soft piano, tender resolve]
There's an empty chair, but there's still time to pull yours close
Listen, just listen, that's how you save the most
